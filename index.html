Our Story

There is nothing complicated about what we do. We source palm oil — the kind that has fed families, fueled kitchens, and built livelihoods across Nigeria for generations. What we have chosen to complicate, deliberately, is the standard of how it reaches you.

Synthova Oil was built on a straightforward conviction: that the most essential products deserve the most rigorous care. Palm oil is not a luxury. It is a staple — present at the base of nearly every meal that matters in this part of the world. And yet, for too long, the experience of sourcing it has been defined by inconsistency. Variable quality. Uncertain supply. No guarantee that what you ordered today will match what you received last time.

We exist to close that gap. Every batch of Synthova Oil is handled with an attention to process that most people reserve for premium imports. We are deliberate about sourcing, deliberate about storage, and deliberate about delivery — because we believe that when something shows up in your kitchen, your business, or your production line, it should show up right.

Our name carries that intention. Synthova is about synthesis — the meeting point of quality and reliability, of local roots and modern standards, of what Nigerian agriculture has always been capable of and what it is finally being allowed to become. We are not here to reinvent palm oil. We are here to honour it.

Whether you are a household buying for the week, a food vendor running a tight operation, or a business procuring at scale, Synthova Oil is built to serve you without friction. Consistent product. Honest pricing. A supply you can plan around. This is what we mean when we say we take it seriously.
